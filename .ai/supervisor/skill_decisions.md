# Skill and Workflow Evolution Decisions

Durable supervisor decisions about skill creation, workflow-template changes, scripts, and rejected or deferred suggestions.

## Decision Principles

- Workers report friction and propose skills.
- Reviewers assess whether the issue is real, recurring, and non-duplicative.
- The Codex supervisor decides the smallest durable fix.
- Project-specific knowledge belongs in the project repository.
- Generally reusable scientific-coding workflow knowledge belongs in the AI workflow repository.
- Major workflow or architecture changes wait for milestone-level human review.
